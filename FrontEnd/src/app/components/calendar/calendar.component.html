Sync to Phone

Subscribe to this calendar on your mobile device to see updates automatically.

iPhone (iOS)

  1. Open Settings > Calendar.
  2. Tap Accounts > Add Account.
  3. Select Other > Add Subscribed Calendar.
  4. Paste the link above and tap Next.

Android (Google)

  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer.
  2. Next to "Other calendars", click + > From URL.
  3. Paste the link and click Add calendar.
  4. On your phone, ensure "Sync" is enabled for this calendar.
